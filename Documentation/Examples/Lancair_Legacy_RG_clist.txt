# EMERGENCY AND ABNORMAL
################################################################
sw_checklist:ENGINE FAILURE - TAKEOFF OR LOW
sw_itemvoid:Land ahead if the runway allows|Below about 700 ft pick a site within 30 deg of the nose
sw_item:Best glide|120 kts
sw_item:Emergency|DECLARE
sw_item:Fuel pump|ON
sw_item:Fuel selector|FULLEST TANK
sw_item:Mixture|RICH
sw_item:Mags|CYCLE
sw_item_details:Then back to BOTH
sw_item:Flaps|FULL
sw_item_details:On final

sw_checklist:ENGINE FAILURE - IN FLIGHT
sw_item:Best glide|120 kts
sw_item:Landing site|PICK
sw_item:Fuel pump|ON
sw_item_details:Low boost, or high boost if none fitted
sw_item:Fuel selector|FULLEST TANK
sw_item:Mixture|RICH
sw_item:Mags|CYCLE
sw_item_details:Then back to BOTH
sw_item:Starter|ENGAGE
sw_item_details:If the propeller has stopped
sw_item:Emergency|DECLARE
sw_item:Position|CALL
sw_item_details:On the active frequency or 121.5
sw_item:Transponder|7700

sw_checklist:ROUGH ENGINE
sw_item:Mixture|RICH
sw_item:Power|REDUCE
sw_item_details:About 2100 RPM, if still rough
sw_item:Mags|L, BOTH, R, BEST
sw_item:Mixture|READJUST
sw_itemvoid:If the cause is unclear|Land at the nearest airport

sw_checklist:ENGINE FIRE - IN FLIGHT
sw_itemvoid:Acrid smell|Treat as electrical first
sw_item:Avionics master|OFF
sw_item:Master|OFF
sw_item:Radios and lights|OFF
sw_itemvoid:If the smell clears|Master ON, then one item at a time. Land soon
sw_item_heading:If Fire Continues|center
sw_item:Throttle|IDLE
sw_item:Mixture|CUT-OFF
sw_item:Fuel shutoff valve|OFF
sw_item:Fuel pump|OFF
sw_item:Transponder|7700
sw_item:Radio|EMERGENCY
sw_item_details:Call "emergency" and position
sw_item:Land|IMMEDIATELY
sw_item_details:Exit the aircraft

sw_checklist:SPEED BRAKE FAULT
sw_item:Speed brake breaker|PULL
sw_item_details:Retracts them
sw_itemvoid:Never use in icing|Expect a higher sink rate if one stays out

sw_itemvoid:Do not open the canopy
sw_item:Airspeed|REDUCE

sw_item_details:If it will not lock, land as soon as practical
